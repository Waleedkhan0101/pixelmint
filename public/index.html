SUPPORT

Frequently asked questions

What metadata is removed?

Python decodes the image and exports a fresh pixel buffer without copying source EXIF, GPS, XMP, comments or PNG text. The exported file is checked for these fields. Standard encoding headers may remain.

Does this remove AI labels or guarantee uploads?

No. Platforms may detect AI-generated content through visual analysis, watermarks, provenance records or other signals. Re-encoding cannot guarantee removal of every signal or acceptance by any service. Follow the platform's disclosure and upload rules.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

When deployed, images are sent to the Vercel-hosted Python service and returned after processing. The application does not store images on disk or in a database. When running locally, images go to your local Python service.

Will resizing affect image quality?

Upscaling does not create new detail. JPG and WEBP quality can change with compression. PNG export is lossless after resizing, and its quality slider is not used. Fit inside preserves the full image with padding when needed.

What are the file limits?

Up to 10 JPG, PNG and WEBP files, 4 MB each (input and output), 40 MB total input, with dimensions no larger than 8192 pixels per side and up to 32 megapixels for source and output. Animated files are rejected; very large images may exceed your device's memory.
