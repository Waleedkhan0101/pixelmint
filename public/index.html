Frequently asked questions

What metadata is removed?

Images are re-encoded without deliberately copying source EXIF, GPS, XMP or comments. Standard encoding headers may remain.

Does this remove AI labels?

No guarantee. Platforms can use visual analysis, watermarks and provenance records to identify AI-generated content. Follow their disclosure requirements.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

Images are processed locally in your browser. Automatic background removal downloads model files from external providers on first use.

Will upscaling create new image details?

Upscaling increases resolution through resampling. It does not generate new detail.

What are the file limits?

10 images per batch, 20 MB per input, 100 MB total input and 200 MB total export. Maximum dimensions are 8192 pixels per side and 32 megapixels for the batch editor. Device memory may impose lower limits.
